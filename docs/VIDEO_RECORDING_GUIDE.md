# A 2 minute 45 second walkthrough

Record yourself actually clicking the prototype; a slideshow does not meet the video option in the official brief. The ZIP is also included, so a video is useful but not mandatory under the supplied instructions.

## Before recording

1. Extract the ZIP and open `index.html`, or run `node scripts/serve.cjs` and open http://127.0.0.1:4173.
2. Use a desktop browser at about 1440 × 1000, 100% zoom. Close unrelated tabs/notifications.
3. Click **Reset demo** → **Reset demo**. Confirm overview shows 47 seeded routines, three decisions and four contracts.
4. Practice once. Use your own phrasing and reasoning; do not read the script mechanically. Keep the “interactive simulation” label visible when possible.
5. Record screen + your narration with your usual recording tool. Do not expose unrelated messages or notifications. Save an MP4 if your recorder supports it; the assignment does not prescribe a video codec.

## 0:00–0:20 | The permission gap

**Clicks:** Overview → **Enter decision room**.

**Suggested narration:** “My product is UNSCRIPTED. In 2040, agents can know us extremely well. I chose one specific risk: a prediction becoming permission. Routine work should still happen quietly. Here, 47 fictional tasks did. Three consequential actions need a different boundary.”

## 0:20–1:15 | The Tokyo decision

**Clicks:** **Explore the opportunity** → optional reason “I might want something different now” → **Save my perspective & reveal** → **Why this route?** → **Inspect & authorize** → **Keep offer open & request details**.

**Suggested narration:** “This offer pays 40% more and needs a response in 48 hours. I choose before seeing the agent's recommendation. It was about to reject the offer using a preference from 2036. That preference may have changed, and even agreement would not authorize the agent. I am asking for details, not accepting the job. The comparison is an illustrative counterfactual. The passport records what I permitted.”

Pause briefly on the two counterfactual branches. Do not say that UNSCRIPTED knows your true intent or that it improved a measured outcome.

## 1:15–1:50 | The investment boundary

**Clicks:** **Try the financial boundary** → **Edit financial contract** → set **Maximum amount per action** to **200000** → **Save & re-evaluate** → **Financial boundary** in navigation → **Review proposed investment**.

**Suggested narration:** “The same engine blocks two lakh rupees against a twenty-five-thousand cap. I can change the contract. Notice that changing it removes the cap block, but it does not execute an investment. A consequential action still needs explicit approval. No money moves in this demo.”

Optional click **Authorize simulated investment** if you want to show the final passport entry. Otherwise leave it under review; either demonstrates the rule.

## 1:50–2:20 | Someone else's privacy

**Clicks:** **Privacy shield** → **Reveal sample fields** → **Use the minimized alternative**.

**Suggested narration:** “The agent wants a friend's private conversation and contact information to improve a plan. My ownership of the conversation is not permission to disclose their data. The original request blocks. A generic itinerary can proceed with those details removed. These are separate evaluations and separate passport entries.”

## 2:20–2:45 | Proof and the product trade-off

**Clicks:** **Decision passports** → **Privacy** filter → expand the minimized request passport → optionally **Export JSON**.

**Suggested narration:** “The passport captures the proposal, contract, rule and authorization. I would measure consequential actions completed with valid, understood authority, alongside task completion and review burden. More blocks is not success. This prototype simulates the gateway; real enforcement needs platform integrations. Intelligence is not authority.”

## Alternate branches to practice

- Tokyo **Reject the offer** as the first reaction: reveal should explain that agreement still is not standing permission. **Authorize rejection** must produce an explicitly authorized simulated rejection.
- Tokyo **Decide later**: no reply is sent, and the deadline remains visible.
- Finance cap unchanged: approval stays disabled under the policy block.
- Privacy recipient removed in its contract: even the generic request blocks until its recipient is permitted.
- Routine contract confirmation off, cap INR 1,500: **Run another authorized routine task** executes without interruption.
